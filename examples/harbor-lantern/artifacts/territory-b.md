# Territory B — Neighborhood noticeboard

## Thesis
Make the workshop's activity visible through a compact board of classes and repairs.

## Composition
Stacked announcements, large dates, textured rhythm made with original shapes, smaller lantern at the top.

## Why it fits
It can communicate a busy shared place and many reasons to return.

## Tradeoff
Denser material may bury a first visit. Keep this alternative as a documented draft; the fictional lead selects Quiet workbench for clarity.
