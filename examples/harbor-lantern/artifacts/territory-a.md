# Territory A — Quiet workbench

## Thesis
Let a single lantern introduce the workshop while practical information stays prominent.

## Composition
Warm paper, dark blue text, amber accent, one emblem, generous space, visit action beside hours.

## Why it fits
The audience needs a friendly invitation and useful information more than spectacle. Quiet space protects the mark and supports scanning.

## Risk and review question
Could the restrained opening hide the workshop's energy? Test real class listings in the next section.
