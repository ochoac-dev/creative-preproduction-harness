# Harbor Lantern creative brief — version 1

## Audience
Neighbors who want a simple print repair, a workshop visit, or a first class.

## Purpose and desired action
Invite a first visit. Show opening hours, access details, and a clear visit action.

## Desired feeling
Warm, useful, and quietly inventive. A workshop, not a luxury gallery.

## Content priorities
Visit action; opening hours and access; current classes; repair service; workshop story.

## Constraints
Use the original lantern emblem. No stock imagery, tracking services, or custom font download. Preserve native links and meaningful headings.

## Evidence of success
A first-time visitor can find the next action and opening hours without reading the whole story. Confirm this with people during implementation.
