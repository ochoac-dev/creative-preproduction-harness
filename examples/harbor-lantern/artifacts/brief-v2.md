# Harbor Lantern creative brief — version 2

## Audience and purpose
Help neighbors arrange a first visit, repair, or class. Keep the welcoming tone of version 1.

## Revision
Place the visit action and opening hours before the long workshop story at narrow widths. Provide text access instructions rather than relying on the emblem.

## Creative constraints
Use the original lantern SVG, quiet paper space, restrained amber, dark blue text, native fonts, and visible focus states.

## Success evidence
A person can identify opening hours and the visit action on desktop and a narrow phone layout. This remains an implementation test, not an automatic score.
